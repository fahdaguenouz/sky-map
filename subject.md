<h2>Sky map</h2>
<p>A sky map app is a mobile application that provides users with an interactive map of the night sky. It utilizes the device&#39;s sensors, such as the GPS, accelerometer, and magnetometer, to determine the user&#39;s location and orientation. With this information, the app can display a real-time representation of the stars, planets, constellations, and other celestial objects visible from the user&#39;s location.</p>
<h3>Instructions</h3>
<p>To manage states within the app, you should use either the BLoC or Provider pattern.</p>
<p>To complete this exercise, you will need to do the following:</p>
<ul>
<li>Utilize the <code>GPS</code>, <code>accelerometer</code>, and <code>magnetometer</code> sensors to determine the <code>location</code> and <code>position</code> of the device.</li>
<li>Implement <code>real-time updates</code> by updating an image at least <strong><em>10 times per second</em></strong>.</li>
<li>Send a request to a public API to retrieve information about celestial objects or use a valid file.</li>
<li>Display the celestial objects on a black canvas within the app.</li>
<li>Allow users to tap on an object to view a short description of it.</li>
<li>Use either the <code>BLoC</code> or <code>Provider</code> pattern to manage states within the app.</li>
<li>The objects to be displayed in the app include:<ul>
<li>All planets of the solar system</li>
<li>The sun</li>
<li>The moon</li>
<li>At least three constellations of your choice</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>When moving the phone around, the celestial objects shown on the display should change</li>
</ul>
<p>Remember to follow best practices for coding and app development, and be sure to document your code and any decisions made during the development process. Don&#39;t forget to consider user experience and design when creating the app, as a visually appealing and intuitive interface will be important for its success.</p>
